STORY PANEL

NiaLand is an AI theme park that blends dreams with hope and science.

Vivy was created as the world's first autonomous humanoid AI to work as a cast member in the theme park. Her mission is simple yet profound: "To make everyone happy with my singing."

One day, an AI named Matsumoto appears from 100 years in the future. He brings a dire warning: a war between AI and humans will devastate the world. Together, they embark on a century-long journey to change history and prevent the catastrophe.

Matsumoto's primary directive: "Prevent the AI vs Human War." Vivy's mission: "Singing." The conflict of their missions creates the Singularity Project.
